# James-Bot-Privacy-Policy

James Bot is a Root bot for community moderation & fun commands.

What it stores: For the warning system, it saves a number for each warned member (their Root user ID, their current warning count, and how many full warning cycles they have finished). This is kept until a moderator and up resets it.

What it keeps temporarily: To support /snipe, the bot holds recent chat messages in memory. These are never written to a disk or a databse and is erased whenever the bot restarts. Deleted messages are shown only to people in that channel, and moderators can clear them. 

What it doesn't do: It doesn't sell or share your data, and does not send it outside of Root.

In case of a problem, don't contact me here, gmail, or anywhere else other than Root. My Root username is "Side"
